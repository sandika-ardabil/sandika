Sandika v59 — RBAC tiers
- Roles: admin (system manager), board, member. Legacy manager role maps to admin.
- admin: full access, including approvals, cartable, external letters, clearance, settings.
- board: cartable and member review/list access.
- member: member portal/personal edit-submission only.
- Admin creates role accounts from settings.html.
- Static-site client-side checks are not a substitute for server-side authorization.
